Burning Abyss is a black aristocrats-based archetype. Send Burning Abyss creatures to the Graveyard by **any means necessary** and get rewarded with free effects. And Once per turn, you can play 1 Burning Abyss creature from your hand for free.

- Quickly swarm your opponent with cheap and dispensable creatures.
- Summon Dante to bury your opponent under card advantage.
- And use their effect to deal with any situation presented to you.

Burning Abyss aim to be the agressor and constantly force the opponent to react to dangerous board without any breathing room.
