D1 · Proper-summon bypass phrase

Scope: global permission text that makes an otherwise illegal Extra Deck / Ritual movement legal without counting as a proper summon.

Existing rule/evidence: docs/rules/SUMMONING.md:15,19 and docs/keywords/ACTIONS.md:21 require exact phrase ignoring the restrictions of summon (case variants appear as Summon).

Conflict: Exa requests shorter form:

card nekroz - exa:20
… Release it ignoring the restrictions of Summon.
→ … Release it ignoring summoning restrictions.

Also: card instant fusion — ignoring the restrictions of summon

Reason: established long bypass phrase contradicts requested compact canonical form.

Proposal: Replace all card-text and rule-owner occurrences of ignoring the restrictions of summon / … of Summon with ignoring summoning restrictions. Meaning unchanged: movement becomes legal; does not count as proper summon; never inferred — must be printed.

Impact: docs/rules/SUMMONING.md, docs/keywords/ACTIONS.md, related ADRs/tests references; Exa, Instant Fusion, any future bypass lines. Side effect: one global search-replace of a locked legal phrase.

R1 · No a/an before bare zones

Scope: articles before bare canonical zone names in card text.

Evidence: Brionac requests drop of a before Sideboard. Same residue on Unicore and aggregate Brionac:

card nekroz - brionac (aggregate):20
… put onto Field from a Sideboard

card nekroz - unicore:20
Creatures put onto Field from a Sideboard lose all their abilities.

Current rule: ADR 0010 / ZONES / TEMPLATING drop definite the before bare zones. Indefinite a/an not covered.

Proposal: Do not put a or an before a bare canonical zone name. Write from Sideboard, onto Field, from Grave. Keep articles inside larger ordinary noun phrases that are not bare zone labels.

Boundaries: Does not strip articles from non-zone nouns (a Ritual Creature). Complements existing no-the zone style.

Impact: docs/rules/ZONES.md, docs/rules/TEMPLATING.md; Brionac, Unicore, similar Sideboard lines; tests. Side effect: broaden article strip beyond the.

D2 · Event keyword exact casing

Scope: printed casing of documented event keywords.

Existing rule/evidence: docs/keywords/EVENTS.md:48 defines **On Exile**. Ability format requires bold event keyword after prefix. Taxonomy requires canonical capitalization on complete keyword invocations.

Conflict: Exa and Great Sorcerer print lowercase lead-in:

card nekroz - exa:20
<b>on Exile</b> —

card nekroz - great sorcerer:20
<b>on Exile</b> —

Reason: printed form contradicts documented keyword token.

Proposal: Printed event keywords must match the documented token exactly, including Title Case of fixed words. Write **On Exile**, never on Exile, ON EXILE, or mixed forms. Applies to the whole documented event family.

Impact: docs/keywords/EVENTS.md (explicit casing note), TEMPLATING cross-pointer if needed; Exa, Great Sorcerer, any mistyped event leads. Side effect: lint may enforce later.

R2 · Compact Ritual-alone substitute

Scope: reusable wording when one permanent may alone pay a Ritual sacrifice/material requirement for a named family.

Evidence: Shurit long static → compact request:

card nekroz - shurit:19
“Shurit” alone can satisfy the sacrifice cost for a Ritual Summon of a “Nekroz” Ritual Creature.
→ Shurit can satisfy Ritual sacrifice of “Nekroz” alone.

Current rule: No compact template. Self-name uses unquoted full printed name (ADR 0009). Archetype quotes retained for “Nekroz”.

Proposal: When one named permanent may alone satisfy Ritual material/sacrifice for a selector family, write: [full self-name] can satisfy Ritual sacrifice of [selector] alone. Example: Shurit can satisfy Ritual sacrifice of *“Nekroz”* alone. Bold Ritual Summon only when the action is performed, not inside this static permission line unless the line itself performs the summon.

Boundaries: Does not change actual Ritual MV math. Does not imply multi-creature payment. Card-specific MV exceptions stay on the card. If wording is archetype-only flavor without reuse, reject and leave MSE-only.

Impact: docs/rules/TEMPLATING.md and/or docs/rules/SUMMONING.md Ritual section; Shurit; future alone-substitute creatures. Side effect: short global template from one strong example.
