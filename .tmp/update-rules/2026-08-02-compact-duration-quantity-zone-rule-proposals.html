D1 · Same-turn duration shorthand

Scope: global temporary-duration syntax for effects that last until the current turn ends.

Existing rule/evidence: docs/rules/TEMPLATING.md:83 canonical form uses until the end of the turn. ADR 0007 locked ability-loss + P/T with that phrase. Corpus also mixes MTG-like until end of turn (Spellbook Power/Wisdom tests and MSE).

Conflict: Alich proposes ... becomes 0/1 to end turn; Effect Veiler proposes ability loss to end turn. Current MSE still prints long form:

card burning abyss - alich:21
… becomes 0/1 until the end of the turn.

card effect veiler:18
… until the end of the turn, it loses all abilities…

Reason: established long form contradicts requested compact duration token used on multiple cards.

Proposal: Write same-turn end duration as to end turn. Replace until the end of the turn and bare until end of turn in card text and templates. Keep longer phrases only when duration is not the current turn (example: until the end of the opponent’s next turn).

Impact: docs/rules/TEMPLATING.md; Alich, Effect Veiler, Clausolas, Dante, Maxx C, many +P/+T effects; related tests. Side effect: denser, less MTG-native prose; multi-turn durations stay explicit.

R1 · Default object is card — omit bare card

Scope: compact action quantities and post-selection counting phrases.

Evidence: Draghig shortens Discard 1 card, then Draw 1 card → Discard 1 then Draw 1. Barbar shortens for each card exiled → for each exiled. Spellbook Knowledge already uses Draw 2 without card (ADR 0004). Phoenix Wing / Karma Cut tests already expect Discard 1.

card burning abyss - draghig:21
… Discard 1 card, then Draw 1 card.

card burning abyss - barbar:21
… for each card exiled.

Current rule: Named/archetype selectors omit generic card(s) (TEMPLATING.md Selectors). No rule covers bare Draw N/Discard N or counting anaphors. EVENTS.md:22 still shows **Draw** 1 card **On Opponent Creature Enter**.

Proposal: Default object of a quantity action or counting anaphor is a card. Omit bare card/cards after governing quantities and after a prior card selection already in scope. Canonical forms: **Draw** 1, **Discard** 1, for each exiled, for each milled. Keep an explicit noun when it restricts or disambiguates: type/qualifier (Creature, nonland permanent), named/archetype selector, structural noun (top card of Deck), or game term where card is not the default object (card type).

Boundaries: Does not erase type words. Does not force dropping commas that mark ordered/dependent steps (then/if you do still govern order). Soft style only: drop commas that add no order/dependency signal.

Impact: docs/rules/TEMPLATING.md, docs/keywords/EVENTS.md, action examples; Draghig, Barbar, Maxx C, Mathematician, Dante Pilgrim, Allure, Upstart, Twin Twisters, tests. Side effect: for each exiled is denser English; future normalization must keep restrictive nouns.

Already settled nearby: unqualified from Grave (no your) remains owned by ZONES.md / ADR 0004 — not reopened here. Barbar’s from your Grave is normalization debt under that existing rule.

D2 · No article before bare zone names

Scope: prepositional zone references in card text and rule templates.

Existing rule/evidence: Canonical zone names are bare initials-uppercase terms (ZONES.md). Newer prose already prefers article-free forms: on Stack, on Field, from Grave (ACTIONS.md, CARD_TYPES.md, ZONES.md:20). Conflict remains in TEMPLATING.md:85 (on the Field) and many MSE lines.

Conflict: Effect Veiler shortens on the Stack → on Stack, and generalizes to all zones.

card effect veiler:18
… Counter all its abilities on the Stack.

TEMPLATING.md:85
Ability loss on the Field ends when card leaves Field.

Reason: established template article contradicts compact zone style already half-adopted in keyword docs.

Proposal: In card text and reusable templates, do not put the before a bare canonical zone name. Write on Stack, on Field, from Grave, into Hand, from Deck. Keep the only inside larger ordinary noun phrases that are not bare zone labels (example: the top card of Deck keeps the top card; zone stays bare).

Impact: docs/rules/TEMPLATING.md, zone examples in ACTIONS.md/EVENTS.md/CARD_TYPES.md; Effect Veiler, Bagooska, Back Jack, Super Polymerization, Crane Crane, Tour Guide, many Field/Stack lines; tests. Side effect: broad future normalization across zone prepositions.
