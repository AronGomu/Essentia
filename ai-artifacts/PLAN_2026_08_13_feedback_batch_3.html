Item 4 — closed, not built

The premise did not survive measurement. Splitting blog, docs and cards into three builds assumed a large shared rebuild cost. Astro's share of the 109 s is 1.5 s; the entire content build with image writes skipped is 0.66 s. After T1 there is nothing left to split, so no --scope flag and no second Astro config ship. Recorded in docs/ADR/proposed/0039-one-astro-app.md.

Residual risk, stated plainly

CI has no warm cache. At AVIF effort 2 a cold build costs roughly 1.4 s per card, so CI stays near a minute at 50 cards and grows linearly — and T2 deliberately adds a second cold build to prove the cache is honest. Parallelising the encode across the 16 available cores is out of scope by decision, and is the obvious next lever if CI time becomes the complaint.
