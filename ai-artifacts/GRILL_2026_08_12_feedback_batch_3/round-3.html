What the corpus actually contains

  • Archetype names in rule text are always quoted, with curly quotes: Search 1 “Nekroz” Creature, Sacrifice 2 “Burning Abyss” Creatures. So a name-reference rule is precise and cheap — no fuzzy matching needed.
  • 22 cards name their own archetype. Zero cards name an archetype they are not in. The relation you described therefore renders nothing at all today; it starts paying off the first time you print a generic card that says Search 1 “Nekroz” Creature.
  • Tour Guide is not caught by it either. Its text is Summon 1 Fiend MV 1 Creature — a subtype, not a name. Its Burning Abyss link stays what it already is: the authored archetype: "burning-abyss" field in identities.json.
  • Every quoted token in the corpus: Ash Blossom · Barbar · Brionac · Burning Abyss · C · Catastor · Clausolas · Dante · Decisive Armor · Gungnir · Maxx · Nekroz · Rubic · Shurit · Trishula · Unicore · Valkyrus. Two are archetypes; the rest are individual card names, nearly all self-references.
  • Growth is linear, not quadratic: one edge per quoted name per card, bounded by how many names you print. This is the property the old rule lacked.
1. A card names an archetype it is not in — link to the archetype, or to every member?

A card saying Search 1 “Nekroz” Creature can produce one tile pointing at the Nekroz page, or 22 card tiles. This is the decision that keeps the new relation linear.

2. Does the referenced side show it back?

A Nekroz player looking at a Nekroz card arguably wants to know “what outside cards can fetch this”. That is the reverse edge, and it is where most of the value sits.

3. Quoted card names — do they create relations too?

The corpus quotes 15 individual card names, nearly all self-references (Discard “Trishula”). A card naming another specific card is currently rare but entirely legal.

4. Root-level docs and the /docs/ landing page

You said root docs come first, alphabetically. Today docs/PRESENTATION.md is special-cased to be the /docs/ page itself; with numbers now in URLs that special case has to be re-decided.

5. How is a group's visible label derived from its folder name?

The rail says “General rules”; the disk says 01_general_rules. One deterministic rule has to turn one into the other, with no per-folder config, or you are back to editing JSON.
