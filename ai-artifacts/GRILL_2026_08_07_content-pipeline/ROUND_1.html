Design the card-to-content pipeline

Three inputs — voice recording, card image, rules-text string — must become one vertical short and one static post from a single terminal command. Sixteen independent decisions form the current frontier. Everything downstream (render engine, file conventions, platform auth, music licensing, caption templates) is blocked until these settle. The environment has already been surveyed — read the two fact panels below before answering, because one of them constrains what is even possible. Tick any recommended answers that fit; the precision box is a free-form custom answer.

1. What does one invocation of the command cover?

Sets the unit of failure and retry. A batch that dies on card 7 of 12 is a different tool than one you run per card.

2. Where does the pipeline live, and in what stack?

Determines whether the pipeline can read card data directly or must be fed an export, and which dependencies you are willing to install.

3. How far does the command go — render only, or publish?

Publishing pulls in OAuth apps, tokens, rate limits and platform review. It is by far the largest hidden scope in this project.

4. What drives the timeline — the audio, or a fixed template length?

The spine of every montage decision. Everything else (beat timing, reveal points, CTA duration) is expressed relative to whatever you pick here.

5. What is the shot structure of the short?

The single largest quality lever, and the thing that decides how capable the render engine has to be. See the storyboard figure above.

6. How does the rules-text string appear on screen?

You listed the text as a separate input, so it must earn its place beyond what is already printed on the card image.

7. Are your spoken words captioned on screen?

Most short-form is watched muted. Burned-in captions are the single biggest measurable retention lever in the format — and they need a transcript your three inputs do not include.

8. How is the voice audio treated, and is there music?

Platform loudness normalisation will flatten anything you don't control. Music is also the one thing here that can get a video muted or a channel struck.

9. How is the call to action expressed in the video?

You named two destinations — the website and the YouTube channel. Two CTAs compete; the frame has to resolve that.

10. One master video for all three platforms, or per-platform cuts?

Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts each cover different parts of the frame with their own UI, and enforce different duration limits. See the frame figure above.

11. What exactly is the static post deliverable?

"A post with the image of the card" can mean the bare render or a composed graphic. Instagram and X also want different aspect ratios.

12. Where does the written post copy come from?

Your three inputs contain no caption. It must be templated, transcribed, or generated — and that decides whether the pipeline needs a network call and an API key.

13. Should every card look identical, or should the template vary?

Identical output builds instant brand recognition across a set; varied output fights viewer fatigue when someone sees five in a row.

14. How do you iterate when a render comes out wrong?

You will not like the first output. The cost of the edit-render-watch loop decides whether this pipeline survives past card three.

15. Which card image is actually the input?

Surveyed fact: renders/*.png is 375×523 px. Placed at native size it covers about a third of a 1080×1920 frame, so every zoom, push-in or card-fills-screen shot in questions 5 and 6 is upscaling by 3× or more. renders_print/*.png is 1500×2092 and would be perfect — but it is documented as optional per package. This decides whether the montage can be sharp at all.

16. What brand mark and typography does the content carry?

Surveyed fact: the repo has no logo file, and Charter/Aptos are referenced as system fonts with no font files checked in — but drawtext and libass both need a concrete font file path, and the CTA needs something to point at. The site's colour system does exist (OKLCH tokens: Relic, Ember, Shadow, Ice, Aether, Archive Gold, Blackfoil).
