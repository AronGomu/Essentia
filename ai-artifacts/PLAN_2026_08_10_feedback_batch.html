Decisions taken while planning

Capenna is normalised, not exempted

744 × 1039 → 750 × 1046: art resampled and every style coordinate scaled by the same 750/744. Acceptance is a side-by-side render check — a shifted title bar means the scale is wrong.

Interaction clauses need an action keyword

Without it, the Xyz material line 2 Creatures MV 1 would relate every MV-1 creature to every Xyz card. The gate is the card's own action / cost-procedure keywords.

Build failure is narrow, on purpose

Only an unresolvable quoted run and a malformed MV token fail the build. whose MV meets its Ritual cost is prose in three Nekroz cards and stays legal; a reference matching zero cards is legal too.

The version route keeps its inline rulings

Only /cards/{id} drops them. The divergence is deliberate and asserted, because a previous accidental divergence is exactly what the old test was guarding against.

Locked packages stay SD

ADR 0008 makes them immutable. Only the one open alpha package gains HD renders and print masters.

Item 10 lands on the homepage

There is no per-archetype card list on the homepage, so "archetype title to cards" is read as the Archetypes heading → tile row gap.

Risks

  • The spike may say "rewrite every coordinate". T12 then grows by four style files. .script/scale_mse_style.py and the side-by-side render check are specified either way.
  • 173 cards have no HD source. T13 skips and reports them; the upscaler is a TODO(user) recorded in MSE/README.md.
  • Header slack at 400px is ~27px. The budget module is updated with measured widths and the e2e overflow assertion is the real gate.
  • Every HD step rewrites render hashes. Expected; package-sha256.json and render-provenance.json churn on T12, T13 and T14.
