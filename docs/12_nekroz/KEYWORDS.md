# Nekroz keywords

## Nekroz Recovery

**Nekroz Recovery** means: “If you control no creatures: **Exile** this card and 1 other *“Nekroz”* from Grave; **Search** 1 non-Creature **Ritual Summon** *“Nekroz”*.”

Non-Nekroz cards sharing effect print full text after em dash.

Shared Ritual, Grave, Exile, Search, and Sideboard terms live in global modules.
