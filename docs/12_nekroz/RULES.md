# Nekroz rules

## Naming

Cube display names use `Nekroz - [card name]`. Full official names remain in `original_cards/`.

## Ritual restriction

Nekroz Ritual Creatures may be cast only through **Ritual Summon**.

Unless card says otherwise, each effect of a Nekroz creature is usable once per turn.

Unresolved Ritual MV/Gungnir/Bounce/short-name/Release questions remain proposed in [ADR 0003](../ADR/proposed/0003-nekroz-reconciliation.md). Do not infer decisions from proposed text.
