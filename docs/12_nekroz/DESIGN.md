# Nekroz design

Nekroz is blue **Ritual / Toolbox / Anti-Extra Deck**.

Encourage:

- searching specific pieces;
- converting Hand cards into utility;
- strategic Grave/Exile use;
- punishing Sideboard creatures;
- incremental advantage over immediate lethal combos.

Expected progression: find Ritual spell + creature, stabilize Hand through support, cast first Ritual Creature, control opposing Sideboard threats, recycle Ritual spells while Field is empty, regain advantage through recurring engine, finish with resilient Ritual threats.

Future cards must maintain Ritual identity, avoid over-efficient normal creatures, give Ritual Creatures useful Hand effects, limit searches, use Sideboard as resource/interaction target, keep blue consistency/tempo/control identity.
