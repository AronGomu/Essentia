# Ability keywords

## Evergreen Magic abilities

Evergreen abilities such as **Flying**, **Trample**, **Vigilance**, **Lifelink**, **Menace**, **Flash**, **Ward N**, **Indestructible**, **Hexproof**, **Protection from everything**, and **Protection from [quality]** appear bold, normally on own line, without numbered Static prefix.

`Flash` inside activated ability metadata remains prefix metadata, not printed evergreen keyword.

## Effect Indestructible

Creature cannot be destroyed by spell or ability. Combat rules or non-effect destruction may still destroy it. Use full **Indestructible** for normal Magic evergreen meaning.

To grant either, write `gains **Indestructible**` or `gains **Effect Indestructible**`.

## Hexproof

Card cannot be targeted by opponents' spells or abilities.

## Bounded N

Card carrying ability is `bounder`. Controller chooses up to N other creatures they control; they become bounded. Bounded benefit lasts only while bounder remains on Field. Bounder leaving ends all links/effects. Bounded permanent leaving allows immediate replacement within limit N.

Archetype-specific abilities live in archetype `KEYWORDS.md` files.
