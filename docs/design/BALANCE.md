# Balance

## Environment

Cube is closed. Evaluate every card against cards available here, not normal Magic Limited.

Target play supports:

- several actions per turn;
- rapid development;
- frequent Extra Deck summons;
- strong interaction;
- recoverable resource exchanges;
- recognizable archetype engines.

## Constraints

- Mana costs restrain Yu-Gi-Oh!-style explosive starts.
- Interaction must prevent infinite loops and non-interactive endings.
- Search and recursion need meaningful tempo/resource costs.
- Summon shortcuts must respect [proper-summon rules](../rules/SUMMONING.md).
- Compact templating cannot change PSCT role, target timing, optionality, or cost/resolution boundaries.
- Shared staples must not erase archetype identity.

Archetype-specific colors, play patterns, progression, and constraints live in archetype `DESIGN.md` modules.
