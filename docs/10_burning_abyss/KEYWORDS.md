# Burning Abyss keywords

## Descent

Sorcery-speed activated ability: “If you have not summoned a *“Burning Abyss”* creature this turn, **Summon** this card from Hand.”

## Abyssal Curse

“If you control another creature without *“Burning Abyss”* in its name, **Destroy** this creature.”

Shared **On Send Grave**, Xyz, Synchro, and action definitions live in global keyword/rule modules.
