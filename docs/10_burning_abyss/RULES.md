# Burning Abyss rules

- **Descent** is sorcery-speed summon from Hand. It does not cast card or trigger **On Cast**.
- **Abyssal Curse** appears on every non-Ritual Main Deck Burning Abyss creature. Ritual Creatures are excluded.
- Global proper-summon restrictions apply to Xyz, Synchro, Ritual, Fusion, and Link cards.

Definitions live in [KEYWORDS.md](KEYWORDS.md).
