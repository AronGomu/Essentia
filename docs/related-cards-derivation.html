Related cards derivation

How website/scripts/content/related.mjs turns rule text into two related-card lists at build time. Companion to ADR 0032.

Worked example

Tour Guide From the Underworld — Fiend, MV 1

(1 - Triggered Hard) On Enter — Summon 1 Fiend MV 1 Creature from Hand or Deck.
It loses its abilities on Field.

The two categories

1 · Same archetype

Every other card whose name contains the archetype's namePattern from website/content/sections.json, quote-normalised and case-insensitive.

Printed name, not the archetype field — a support card that does not print the name is not in this list, even though it carries the field.

2 · Interacts with this card

Union over clauses that name one of the card's own action or cost-procedure keywords. A candidate must satisfy every constraint kind present in the clause.

The action-keyword gate is what keeps 2 Creatures MV 1 — an Xyz material line — from relating every MV-1 creature to every Xyz card.

The two lists are disjoint by construction. archetype is computed first, and its ids are subtracted from interaction once that pass runs — an archetype member that fetches its own archetype would otherwise print the same gallery twice.

The subtype vocabulary is read from the catalog, never hardcoded: the cube currently prints Aqua, Bird, Dragon, Fairy, Fiend, Human, Insect, Machine, Psychic, Rock, Warrior, Wizard, Wyrm, Zombie, and a fifteenth race must not need a code change to be seen.

What fails the build

  • A quoted run matching neither a section namePattern nor any card name as a substring. Today's quoted runs include archetype names (“Burning Abyss”, “Nekroz”) and card-name fragments (“Dante”, “Barbar”, “Maxx”) — both resolve.
  • An MV token followed by anything other than an integer, X, or the word meets. whose MV meets its Ritual cost is prose in three Nekroz ritual cards and stays legal.

A reference that resolves to zero cards does not fail: an archetype may have no printed member yet.

Where it lives

  1. website/scripts/content/related.mjs — extractClauses, parseConstraints, buildRelatedGraph.
  2. website/scripts/content/orchestrator.mjs attaches related to every card before writing src/generated/catalog.ts. Schema version 11.
  3. website/src/pages/cards/[id].astro renders two CardGallery grids, 12 cards each.

There is no separate cache file and nothing is computed at runtime: the site is statically built, so the content build is the cache, invalidated by the same card source hashes as everything else in the catalog.
