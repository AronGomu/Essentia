01 Opening

Hi everyone — welcome to the presentation of my beloved project: YGO × MTG: Essentia.

Goal: let you play Yu-Gi-Oh! cards under the Magic rules system, while keeping the feeling of playing YGO.

It started as a Magic cube of adapted YGO cards. It slowly evolved into basically an entire new game derived from Magic’s rules engine.

That happened because rawdogging the cube was not really possible.

The cube problem

YGO deckbuilding is archetype- and synergy-heavy. To pilot a deck you need almost every card and the sequences to chain them. A first-comer to a cube cannot be expected to know those synergies.

So: structure decks per archetype, playable against each other — first exposure before the cube.

  • 6 structure decks · 6 archetypes
  • 50 cards each → 40 main + 10 sideboard
  • Strip basics, add some cards → playable cube for at least 6 players

02 Who I am

But first — who am I?

~2009 · Synchro era

Started YGO early. Weekly locals at Synchro release, age 10. Horrible Synchro structure → bad Zombie Synchro → Lightsworn mix → Blackwing after it fell. Budget kid energy.

HAT → PEPE

Played seriously end of HAT through start of PEPE. Heavy on Dueling Network. IRL budget Burning Abyss. Lived DUEA and Nekroz. Blessed: BA dodged almost every ban in my competitive run.

PEPE stop

Hard stop. Hated 10-minute combo turns into scoop. Loved interactive formats: Shaddoll, Burning Abyss, Nekroz. Still peeked at T0 (Zoodiac, Tearlaments). Certified YGO boomer: “in my time we played more than two turns.”

2016 · Modern golden era

Transitioned to Magic. Jeskai Control and Death’s Shadow into that goaded format. Serious competitive MTG until FIRE design. Retreated to Legacy with a local community.

Hot take (as written)

Disgusted by both games’ directions. FIRE and Commander design encourage non-interactive play and poison formats. YGO never solved the combo issue. Banlist culture is brutal — and somehow people stay.

03 Why I am making this

Since moving to Magic, I always missed YGO’s high power + interaction. Legacy is the closest cousin — still not the same.

Something missing I can’t put in one word: floating effects, insane value, cardboard flying around. Overlaying into an Xyz is so satisfying. MTG doesn’t move enough pieces doing cool stuff in one turn. — search five times, ritual Valkyrus, sac it, empty board for a +1 energy

I also love Magic’s rules: lands, the overall feel of the game.

Experiment: recreate that YGO feeling inside the MTG system. That’s Essentia.

04 Core idea

Foundation is Magic. Mana, colors, stack, combat, familiar card types.

Design target is not normal Limited or Constructed. Closed environment, balanced against itself. Cards are completely outside Magic norms.

Example — Cir

1-mana 3/2, can be free, and when sent to the graveyard from anywhere reanimates another creature. Broken even in Legacy. Here, that’s the norm.

Everything is broken, so nothing is.

One major difference from YGO: cards still have costs. Minimal — often 1 mana — but still a limit. Hope: multi-turn interactive games, closer to Modern than pure YGO turbo.

Goal: YGO feeling on an MTG chassis — not a 1:1 transposition.

05 YGO specifics → MTG

Extra Deck

Behaves like YGO. Fusions, Synchros, Xyz, Links start in the sideboard and summon via their mechanics — for most, putting them into play costs no mana.

Life, Power & Toughness

Note from script: [REDO POWER AND TOUGHNESS — can be more precise]

Scale: every 500 ATK → 1 power, every 500 DEF → 1 toughness (round down).

Cir 1600/1200 → 3/2.

Alternative considered: 80 life, strip two zeros (Cir 16/12) so direct-attack count matches YGO. Rejected for v1 — stay close to MTG stat language.

Levels → Mana

Levels ≠ mana values. Straight level→cost would make the game too long and kill the YGO feel.

YGO LevelMana cost
1–41
5–62
7–93
10–124

Tradeoff: less precision for Synchro/Xyz/Ritual ladders. Levels 1–4 share tools easily. Chose simplicity over fidelity.

Attributes & Color

  • YGO attributes barely matter; Magic colors define the pie.
  • Heavy LIGHT/DARK bias vs cube color balance.
  • Mapping: LIGHT→White, DARK→Black, FIRE→Red, WATER→Blue, EARTH→Green… WIND?
Decision

Vibe-based colors. Color pie and balance secondary to YGO feel. Extra Deck summons ignore color (two DARK → WIND Xyz is normal). Expect wacky colors. Throw at wall; keep what sticks.

FLIP, Traps & face-down

Global rule: any permanent can be played face-down for the same mana. Announce creature vs non-creature; identify correctly.

  • Cannot flip the turn you set it (YGO trap timing).
  • Wait a full turn before activating a trap.
  • Face-down creature: not instant-speed flip; can block / later attack as expected.
  • Flip triggers effects when applicable; survive a turn → flip yourself for flip effects.

First set: no flip effects yet. Shaddoll coming with a pile of them.

06 Problems — and how I solved them

YGO text purge

Hate walls of text. Mix YGO PSCT + MTG keywords; cut dead words. Machine-like at first glance, readable once keywords click. Bold/italic in MSE to pop actions and zones.

Chaining tutors & combo feel

Best fix so far: tutors / search effects cast free. Archetype sealing lets broken search exist if the rest of the package is checked. Nekroz searches and hand-trap-likes: free for now.

Hand-trap interactions

Minimal interaction pieces also free when possible — upside to the player trying to stop the proactive line. Proactive player already has natural advantage.

One-turn kills

  • Minimal direct damage in print.
  • No haste for now (YGO already “has haste”; flooding haste kills the interactive goal).
  • Easy big boards and easy breaks.
  • Summoning sickness does a lot of OTK work; free effects are two-sided.
  • Future: selective haste + maybe “no attack turn 1” like YGO — for now stay inside MTG systems.
Slog risk

OP creatures + always-full boards + blocking → card-advantage slog, decking as wincon, nobody attacks. Aware of it pre-playtest. Tools like 1-mana Raigeki / Dark Hole style clears to reopen races.

Match length

High interaction = many decisions = long games. If each deck sees most of its cards, variance drops (chess-like resource access). Competitive shape may shift: one game per match instead of Bo3 — especially for cube.

Mulligan

Hearthstone-style

Bottom any number from opening hand; draw that many. No further mulligans. Kills shuffle theater, faster starts. No land in 14? Variance. You deserved it.

07 Problems not solved yet

Lands

Modern YGO often wants >40 for consistency. Essentia targets 40 + 10 extra-only side. Even at 1-mana spells, ~13–14 lands → only ~26–27 “real” cards. Tight for YGO engines. Mitigations: Duel Links-style small decks exist; max 2 copies; smaller decks make decking a real clock.

YGO has no land/mana references. Want lands that talk to YGO cards (custom archetype lands). Reduce flood/screw without a second land deck: highly modal lands (cycle land↔spell), with discard/exile costs so you don’t loop them for free in long games. Needs custom design — parked for now.

08 Alpha 0.1 — Legend of the Alpha

Two starter decks. Playtest fuel. Two favorite archetypes.

Deck 1 Burning Abyss

Competitive home deck. All iterations loved.

Tour Guide Graeff Cir Scarm Farfa Dante Virgil Rubic
  • Self-destruct if you control a non-BA creature
  • No “empty S/T” requirement — free-ish from hand, once per turn (anti-flood; may change)
  • GY triggers kept close to originals
  • Extra: mostly Xyz; Virgil as easy Synchro

Deck 2 Nekroz

Ritual mirror of the BA love letter.

Senju Manju Preparation Brionac Clausolas Shurit Trishula Unicorn
  • Search + hand effects: free; paying mana is for playing / ritualizing
  • Rituals work like YGO — need the ritual spell path
  • Ignoring summon restrictions is the only shortcut for Ritual/Fusion/Xyz/Synchro cheats

09 Why these two

Effects stay close enough that BA vs Nekroz should feel like ten years ago. If Essentia can’t make my favorite archetypes work, the project has no point.

10 What I will avoid

No floodgates. No lingering prison effects.

  • Every card we both hate — stays out of Essentia
